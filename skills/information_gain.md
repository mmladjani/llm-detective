# SKILL — information_gain

Before each action, pick the one that most reduces uncertainty about the culprit — not the next item on a checklist.

Ask, for every legal action: if I take it, how much would it change the hypothesis ranking? Prefer actions that open a NEW independent line of evidence (access log, camera, physical trace) over ones that repeat a line you already have. An interview that only restates a known alibi is low information; verifying that alibi against the records is high. Skip decoys and objects that can't tie to the method — they cost a step and tell you nothing. Stop as soon as two independent lines converge on the same suspect; gathering a third redundant confirmation is wasted budget. When two actions look equally useful, take the cheaper one first.
