# SKILL — final_case_synthesis

Assemble the supported conclusion (or an honest non-conclusion).

Before submitting: does the proposed culprit have at least two independent lines? Are action and method established? Are raised questions resolved? In model mode, use `establish_fact` with a collected evidence id and rationale for each inferred field. Address counterevidence and explain why other suspects are weaker. The confidence ranking is your own bookkeeping, not proof: your justified conclusion may differ from its leader. Submit `unresolved` when the collected record cannot separate alternatives. A confident wrong accusation is the worst outcome.
